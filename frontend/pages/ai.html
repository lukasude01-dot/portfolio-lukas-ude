AI & Systems

Technologywith purpose.

AI is a tool, not a strategy. I use it where it makes work faster or better – in research, drafts, variations and automation – and keep people in charge of judgement, voice and quality.

The flow

From idea to execution – connected.

AI does not replace a step. It shortens the distance between them.

  1. 01

    Idea

    The question worth solving.

    AI supports Collecting angles and questions from your audience.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Positioning, message, journey.

    AI supports Structuring research and testing arguments.

  3. 03

    Creation

    Copy, creatives, pages.

    AI supports First drafts and variations – edited by a person.

  4. 04

    System

    Processes that connect the parts.

    AI supports Summaries, routing and repetitive steps.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Launch, observe, improve.

    AI supports Preparing reports – decisions stay human.

Principles

Four rules I work by.

  1. 01

    People decide

    AI prepares. Judgement, final wording and responsibility stay human.

  2. 02

    Process before tool

    First the workflow, then the software. Tools change – a clear process lasts.

  3. 03

    Data with care

    Personal and client data are handled according to GDPR and only where necessary.

  4. 04

    Honest output

    No invented facts, no fake numbers. If something is unclear, it is checked.

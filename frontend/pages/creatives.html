How I write ads

One motive per ad.

  1. 01

    Insight before idea

    Every ad starts with what the audience already feels – not with what the brand wants to say.

  2. 02

    Readable in one second

    The creative carries one thought. Details belong in the primary text and on the landing page.

  3. 03

    Variations that teach

    Each variation tests one motive, so the results say something useful about the audience.
